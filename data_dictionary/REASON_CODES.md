# Adjustment reason codes

| Code | Chinese label | English description |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | 非参数调整/一般相关公告 | Non-parameter or general notice |
| 01 | 节假日安排与节前后调整 | Holiday schedule and pre/post-holiday adjustment |
| 02 | 市场波动下的临时风险控制 | Temporary risk control during market volatility |
| 03 | 连续涨跌停及规则触发扩板 | Rule-triggered expansion after consecutive limit moves |
| 04 | 合约上市、挂牌与参数初始化 | Contract listing and parameter initialization |
| 05 | 到期、交割月及临近交割调整 | Expiry, delivery-month and near-delivery adjustment |
| 06 | 交易/持仓/开仓限额与异常交易管理 | Trading, position and opening limits; abnormal-trading controls |
| 07 | 制度、规则和长期参数标准调整 | Rule or long-term parameter-standard change |
| 08 | 其他特殊风险处置与应急安排 | Other special risk handling or emergency arrangement |
